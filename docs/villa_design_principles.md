Villa Design Principles
------------------------

1. **Simplicity**: The design should be simple and intuitive to use.
2. **Usability**: The design should be easy to use and understand.
3. **Accessibility**: The design should be accessible to all users, including those with disabilities.
4. **Scalability**: The design should be scalable to accommodate future growth.
5. **Security**: The design should be secure and protect user data.
6. **Performance**: The design should be fast and efficient.
7. **Maintainability**: The design should be easy to maintain and update.
